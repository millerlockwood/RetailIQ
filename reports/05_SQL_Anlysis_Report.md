# RetailIQ SQL Business Analysis

---

## Executive Summary

This report summarizes the initial business insights generated from the RetailIQ analytics engine.

The analysis uses summary tables generated from the RetailIQ data warehouse and represents over **66 million** historical retail sales records.

---

# Store Performance

## Observation

California Store 3 generated the highest total unit sales across the retail network.

## Business Impact

This location should receive additional inventory planning attention because demand substantially exceeds most other stores.

Potential reasons include:

- Larger customer base
- Higher traffic
- Regional purchasing behavior
- Store size

---

# State Performance

## Observation

California generated the highest overall sales.

## Business Impact

California should remain a priority market for inventory allocation, promotional campaigns, and forecasting efforts.

---

# Product Performance

## Observation

A relatively small number of products account for a large percentage of total unit sales.

## Business Impact

These products deserve closer inventory monitoring since stockouts would have the greatest impact on revenue.

---

# Category Performance

## Observation

Category-level sales reveal where customer demand is concentrated.

## Business Impact

Marketing investment and purchasing decisions should prioritize the strongest performing categories.

---

# Monthly Sales

## Observation

Monthly demand changes throughout the year.

## Business Impact

Understanding seasonality improves:

- Purchasing
- Staffing
- Inventory planning
- Forecasting accuracy
